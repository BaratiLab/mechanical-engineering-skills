# Process selection by volume and geometry (rough guide)

| Annual volume | Prismatic metal | Thin-wall plastic | Sheet-like | Complex internal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–50 | CNC | CNC / SLA / MJF | laser cut + brake | AM (metal or polymer) |
| 50–1,000 | CNC | urethane casting / MJF | laser + brake | AM or investment casting |
| 1k–10k | CNC / die cast | aluminum-tool injection molding | turret + brake / progressive die | investment casting |
| > 10k | die cast + finish machining | steel-tool injection molding | progressive / stamping | die cast + cores |

Tooling rough costs: aluminum injection mold $3k–15k; steel mold $15k–100k+; die-casting die $20k–150k; progressive die $20k–200k.
